About

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, which I joined in August 2025. I am a member of the NICE Lab, advised by Prof. Tony Tae-Hyoung Kim. My research focuses on computer architecture and algorithm–hardware co-design for energy-efficient machine learning, spanning domain-specific accelerators and computing-in-memory.

Before NTU, I earned my B.S. in Electronic and Electrical Engineering from Ewha Womans University. As an undergraduate researcher, I designed analog and digital computing-in-memory circuits for MAC acceleration and studied Lissajous scanning for MEMS LiDAR mirrors. I also designed CMOS inverter transimpedance amplifiers through tapeout, and was a research intern at HyperAccel. Outside the lab, I founded XLetter, an interactive web novel platform.

Research interests Algorithm–Hardware Co-Design Microarchitecture Domain-Specific Accelerators for ML Computing-in-Memory
